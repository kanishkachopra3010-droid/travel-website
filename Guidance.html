Detailed Destination Guides

Destination 1: Kyoto, Japan

Kyoto served as Japan's capital and is home to thousands of classical Buddhist temples.

Places to Visit:

  • Historic Temples & Shrines
    • Fushimi Inari Shrine (Torii gates)
    • Kinkaku-ji (The Golden Pavilion)
  • Arashiyama Bamboo Grove

Must-Try Food:

  1. Kaiseki traditional multi-course dinner
  2. Matcha green tea sweets
  3. Yudofu (tofu hot pot)

Travel Tip: Rent a bicycle to easily navigate through the historic districts.

Destination 2: Santorini, Greece

A volcanic island in the Cyclades group of the Greek islands.

Places to Visit:

  • Oia Village (Famous sunset spot)
  • Akrotiri Archaeological Site
  • Unique Volcanic Beaches
    • Red Beach
    • Black Sand Beach in Perissa

Must-Try Food:

  1. Tomato fritters (Tomatokeftedes)
  2. Fava bean puree
  3. Fresh grilled seafood

Travel Tip: Wear comfortable walking shoes, as the cliffside paths are steep and cobbled.

Destination 3: Swiss Alps, Switzerland

A majestic mountain range offering world-class outdoor recreation.

Places to Visit:

  • Matterhorn peak in Zermatt
  • Jungfraujoch (Top of Europe)
  • Scenic Lakes
    • Lake Geneva
    • Lake Lucerne

Must-Try Food:

  1. Traditional Swiss Cheese Fondue
  2. Authentic Swiss Chocolate
  3. Rosti (crispy potato dish)

Travel Tip: Purchase a Swiss Travel Pass for unlimited travel on trains, buses, and boats.
